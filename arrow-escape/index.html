Privacy Policy — Arrow Escape

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Arrow Escape does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, hearts, hints, power-up charges, daily-challenge streak, blitz-mode high score and display settings are stored locally on your device and never uploaded. We run no server for this app.

Advertising

Arrow Escape uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (watching one refills your hearts or grants a hint). AdMob may collect your advertising ID and device information to serve and measure these ads, under Google's own ad policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent before any personalised ad is shown; you can change that choice at any time in your device's ad settings. Ads are optional — nothing in the game requires watching one.

Third parties

Arrow Escape does not use analytics, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. The only third party is Google AdMob, as described above.
