Privacy Policy — Kili Uploader

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Kili Uploader is a private automation run by Marco Stang. It publishes promotional videos for the Kili Josiyam app to the Kili YouTube channel, which Marco Stang owns and operates. It is not a public service: nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the automation receives an access token for the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses that token only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and visibility. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other Google users.

Where the token is kept

The token is stored encrypted in a self-hosted n8n instance operated by Marco Stang. It is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone else.

How to revoke access

The channel owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account under Third-party apps & services.
