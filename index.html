Privacy Policy — Kili

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Kili does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your draw history and app preferences are stored locally via the browser's localStorage and never uploaded.

Personal reading

If you buy the personal reading, the birth date (and time, if you give it), name (if you give it), the card drawn, the chosen life area, and your written question are sent securely (HTTPS) to our server, which passes them to Anthropic, the AI provider, solely to generate your reading text. This data is not stored after the reading is generated — each request is processed and discarded, nothing is saved on our server. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that processing: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

Kili uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Google processes and stores payment information under its own Play Store terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Advertising

Kili uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads (watching one grants extra "karma" in the app). AdMob may collect your advertising ID and device information to serve and measure these ads, under Google's own ad policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Ads are optional — nothing in the app requires watching one.

Third parties

Kili does not use analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what is listed above (Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, and — only for the personal reading — Anthropic).
