Privacy Policy — Kili

Last updated: 08 August 2026

Kili does not require an account and does not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

Your draw history and app preferences (stored locally via the browser's localStorage, never uploaded). Birth date/time entered for the personal reading — used only to compute your birth Nakshatra on your device, never transmitted or stored anywhere.

Purchases

Kili uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Google processes and stores payment information under its own Play Store terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Third parties

Kili does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
